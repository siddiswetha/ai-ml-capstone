# Billing: duplicate or wrong charge

If you were charged twice or the wrong amount, check your order page first.
A charge appears twice when a payment was retried after a slow connection.
To fix it: note the order number and the two charge amounts, then ask support
to refund the duplicate. A refund to the same card takes three to five working
days. Never share your full card number in a ticket; the last four digits are enough.
