# Defect: broken or damaged product

If your product arrived broken, cracked, faulty or damaged, or it stopped
working, take a photo of the damage and keep the packaging. Do not try to
repair it yourself, as that can void the cover. Tell support the order
number, what is broken, and when it stopped working. A replacement or a
refund is offered after the photo is reviewed, usually within two working
days. Keep the product until support tells you whether to return it.
