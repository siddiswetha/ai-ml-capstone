# Account: change email, address or phone

To change the email, address or phone number on your account, sign in and
open your profile page, then edit the field and save. The change applies to
future orders only; an order that has already shipped keeps its old
delivery address. If you cannot sign in, tell support the old and new
details and the order number of a recent order so they can confirm it is
your account. Closing an account takes effect at once and cannot be undone.
